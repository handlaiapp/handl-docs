How handl. handles your information

handl. is a personal assistant that places real phone calls, sends messages, and completes tasks on your behalf. Doing that well means holding some genuinely sensitive information — this page explains exactly what, why, and who else ever sees it.

01

Information we collect

Profile

Your name, date of birth, country of residence, email addresses and phone numbers (you can save several of each, with one marked as default), and — only if you choose to add it — your passport number and expiry date, seat preference, and any airline loyalty numbers.

Payment

Card details are handled and stored by Stripe, our payment processor — handl. itself never sees or stores your full card number. Subscription status is managed by RevenueCat.

Device permissions

Only ever accessed after you grant them, and only for the purpose stated when you're asked:

  • Microphone — to turn a spoken task into text
  • Camera & Photos — to scan a receipt or letter
  • Calendar — to check for clashes and add confirmed bookings
  • Contacts — so you can pick someone for handl. to message or call
  • Location — to prioritise nearby places and answer location-aware questions
  • Face ID / passcode — to lock the app; your biometric data is processed entirely on-device by Apple or Google and never reaches our servers

Connected accounts

If you choose to connect them: read-only access to recent Gmail/Outlook inbox metadata (sender, subject, and a short snippet — never the full message body) to build your daily digest, plus permission to create draft emails on your behalf; and, on the Business plan, Xero access to create draft bills from scanned receipts.

Task activity

The tasks and commands you give handl., recordings and transcripts of calls it places for you, documents you scan and the details extracted from them, and records of bookings, reminders, watched items, and subscriptions you ask it to track.

02

How we use it

To do what you ask: placing calls, sending messages, completing bookings, drafting emails, building your inbox digest and weekly briefing, processing payments, sending status notifications, and generally making handl.'s automated actions more accurate and reliable over time.

03

How we share it

With businesses you ask us to contact

When handl. calls or messages a business on your behalf, it shares only the minimum needed to complete the request — typically just your name, phone number, and email address.

With service providers

Each of the following only ever receives what it needs to do its specific job:

Anthropic (Claude)
Understands your commands and scanned documents
OpenAI (Whisper)
Transcribes voice input
Vapi
Places and manages calls for you
Duffel
Searches and books flights
Stripe
Processes payments
RevenueCat
Manages your subscription
Google Places
Looks up business details
Google / Microsoft / Xero
Only if you connect these yourself

We do not sell your personal information to anyone, for any reason.

04

Storage & security

Your data is stored on servers we operate, protected with encrypted transmission (HTTPS) and access controls. Your passport number is stored securely and only ever displayed in masked form within the app.

05

Your choices

  • Update or delete your profile, passport number, or any saved contact or business at any time in the app
  • Disconnect Gmail, Outlook, or Xero at any time from Settings
  • Turn off App Lock or any individual device permission at any time in your device settings
  • Request a copy of your data, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us below
06

Children's privacy

handl. is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
